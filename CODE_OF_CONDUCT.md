# Community conduct

Help keep this community welcoming and useful:

- Treat contributors and users respectfully, including when you disagree.
- Give constructive technical feedback. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate,
  or publish another person's private information.
- Do not post credentials, customer data, or sensitive model metadata.
- Follow the [security policy](./SECURITY.md) for private vulnerability reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed. To request a private channel for a conduct concern, open an issue
without personal details or sensitive attachments.

This community is maintained independently. Microsoft moderation contacts in
the upstream starter are not the reporting channel for this repository.
